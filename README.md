Step 1: Set Up Free Email Forwarding in Squarespace

This ensures that any email sent to your custom domain (like info@techhelpto.com) automatically lands in your Gmail inbox.
1. Go to your Squarespace Account Dashboard and click on your website or domain panel.
2. Select Domains, then click on your registered domain: techhelpto.com.
3. Click on Email (or Email Forwarding depending on your dashboard view).
4. In the Email Forwarding section, click Add Rule.
5. Fill out the fields:
	• Forward from: Enter the username you want (e.g., info, hello, or your name).
	• Forward to: Enter your Gmail address: valvaatty@gmail.com.
6. Click Save or Add.
7. Check your Gmail inbox for a confirmation email from Squarespace and click the verification link to activate it.

Step 2: Configure Gmail to "Send As" Your Custom Email

To reply to your clients or users from your custom domain instead of your personal Gmail account, you need to link them. Because Google requires an SMTP server to send mail securely, you can use Gmail's own secure servers to handle this by generating an App Password.

Part A: Generate an App Password in your Google Account

1. Go to your Google Account Settings.
2. Make sure 2-Step Verification is turned ON under the Security tab (Google requires this for security).
3. Search the search bar at the top of your Google Account page for "App passwords".
4. Give the password a name (e.g., "Squarespace Custom Email") and click Create.
5. Copy the 16-character code that appears on your screen. You will need this in the next step.

Part B: Add the Email to Gmail Settings

1. Open your Gmail inbox and click the Gear icon (Settings) in the top right corner → click See all settings.
2. Go to the Accounts and Import tab.
3. Scroll down to the Send mail as section and click Add another email address.
4. A yellow pop-up window will appear:
	• Name: Your name or business name.
	• Email address: Your new custom domain email (e.g., info@techhelpto.com).
	• Uncheck the box that says "Treat as an alias."
	• Click Next Step.
5. Enter the SMTP Server details exactly like this:
	• SMTP Server: smtp.gmail.com
	• Port: 587
	• Username: Your full Gmail address (valvaatty@gmail.com)
	• Password: Paste the 16-character App Password you copied in Part A (do not use your regular Gmail login password).
	• Select Secured connection using TLS.
6. Click Add Account.

Part C: Confirm the Setup

1. Google will send a verification code to your custom email. Because you set up forwarding in Step 1, this code will arrive right in your regular Gmail inbox.
2. Copy the verification code from the email, paste it into the yellow pop-up window, and click Verify.

How to use it

Now, whenever you compose a new email or hit reply inside Gmail, you will see a dropdown menu in the "From" field. You can click it to switch between your personal address and your professional techhelpto.com email address.
